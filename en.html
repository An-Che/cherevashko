Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):

How much does SEO cost and how is the price calculated? ▼

I work on an hourly basis, and the final cost is calculated individually after an audit of the project. It consists of two parts:
1. Specialist's work. We determine the necessary number of hours per month. The hourly rate depends on the complexity of the tasks.
2. Link building budget. This is budgeted separately.
You don't overpay for templated packages; you only pay for actual hours and a strategy customized to your goals.

What is AI Optimization (AEO/GEO) and why is it necessary? ▼

Traditional search is changing: users increasingly get direct answers from built-in AI assistants (AI Overviews in Google, ChatGPT, Gemini). AEO/GEO optimization allows you to structure the website content so that AI algorithms read it as an authoritative primary source and recommend your site to users.

What are the guarantees and timeframes for the first results? ▼

The initial progress from technical fixes and basic optimization is visible within 1.5–3 months (see case studies), but the speed depends on the specifics of the niche.

Significant growth in organic traffic, rankings, and inclusion in AI answers takes shape within 4–6 months. It's important to understand: this is the result of comprehensive work and a competitive budget, not just expecting a miracle from editing meta tags.

In SEO, it is impossible to guarantee a specific position: search algorithms constantly change, and personalization shows each user a different picture (metrics only display the average position). From my side, I give a 100% guarantee on expertise and the professionalism of all work performed.
